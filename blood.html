O- O+ A+ B+ AB-

Basic Donor Eligibility Checklist

  1. Age: 18 to 65 years old (16–17 allowed with written parental consent).
  2. Weight: At least 50 kg (110 lbs).
  3. Health Status: Generally in good health with no fever, cough, or colds on donation day.
  4. Rest & Meal: At least 6 hours of sleep and a light, non-fatty meal prior to donating.
  5. Interval: At least 3 months (90 days) since your last whole blood donation.
  6. Valid ID: Bring at least one government-issued or school/company photo ID.

4-Step Donation Process

  1. Registration: Present your valid ID and complete the donor health history form.
  2. Health Screening: Quick check of weight, blood pressure, pulse, and hemoglobin levels.
  3. Blood Collection: Sterile, safe collection taking only 8 to 10 minutes.
  4. Rest & Refreshments: Relax for 10–15 minutes with complimentary snacks and fluids before resuming light activities.
